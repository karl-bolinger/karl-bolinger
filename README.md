## Problems I work on

### Fragmented requests and unclear ownership

**Problem:** Work arrives through chat, email, forms, and service platforms, but teams lack a consistent way to classify, prioritize, and track it.

**Approach:** Define intake taxonomies, ownership and triage rules, workflow states, integration points, and observable handoffs across enterprise systems.

**What this makes possible:** A traceable path from request to decision to delivery, rather than a collection of disconnected queues.

### Cross-functional processes that depend on manual coordination

**Problem:** Onboarding, product lifecycle activities, approvals, and handoffs often live in documents and individual people's knowledge.

**Approach:** Model the process, clarify responsibilities, and design repeatable task templates, approval steps, permissions, and automation using platforms such as Jira and Confluence.

**What this makes possible:** Consistent execution with less ambiguity about the next step, accountable owner, or required evidence.

### AI experimentation without a sustainable operating model

**Problem:** An AI pilot can look promising while access, sensitive data handling, ownership, evaluation, and adoption remain unresolved.

**Approach:** Start with specific use cases, define controls and success criteria, assess the data and integration boundaries, and establish an approach to rollout and feedback.

**What this makes possible:** AI capabilities tied to actual work, with clearer governance and a path to operational use.
